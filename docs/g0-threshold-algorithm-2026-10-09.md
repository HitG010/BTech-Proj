# G0: a bounded confidence-threshold acquisition adaptation

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: specification and synthetic implementation work only.
Read with the [focused plan](novelty-plan-2026-10-08.md) and its
[literature assessment](novelty-literature-2026-10-08.md). No real question,
response, reference, protected outcome, model or completed-study score is an
input to this specification. No scientific result is asserted here.

The proposed procedure is an **empirical adaptation**. Threshold policies,
disagreement sampling, repeat selection, information per acquisition cost,
sampling correction, model-assisted survey estimation and shared feedback are
established ideas. The equations below specialize them to a fixed direct draft
and a finite cache of thinking draws. They do not establish method priority.

## 1. The finite experiment and its estimands

There are `N` training question groups, one fixed direct state per group, and
`R=2` exchangeable independently seeded thinking cells per state. A state is
`(question_id, direct_job_id, direct_response, c_i)`; another direct response is
another state, never another draw of this state. Thinking receives the question
only. Reuse of one thinking response across states is outside this contract.

All training direct outputs, confidence values, direct correctness `D_i` and
direct charged tokens `C_Di` are exposed together and charged to every method.
The scorer/reference endpoint needed to create a real sidecar is a separate
future protocol. Synthetic direct correctness is generated by the synthetic
DGP. Training thinking correctness and length remain private until a purchase.

Let `beta=.05/1000`, `w_i=1/N`, and `j=(i,r)`. Write

```text
G_j = Y_j - D_i - beta*C_j
v_j = w_i/R
B_D = sum_i w_i*(D_i - beta*C_Di)
J_cache(tau) = B_D + sum_j v_j*a_tau(i)*G_j
a_tau(i) = 1[c_i < tau]
J_cache(tau)-J_cache(eta) = sum_j v_j*(a_tau(i)-a_eta(i))*G_j.
```

`J_cache` is the finite, fully enumerated cache estimand, conditional on its
fixed direct states and generated thinking cells. Unequal acquired repeat
counts do not change `w_i` or `v_j`. If a future cache has `R_i` cells per state,
the same formulas use `v_j=w_i/R_i`; that change must be frozen prospectively.

An average over independently generated caches estimates fixed-state expected
solver utility. An independent set of evaluation questions estimates the
selected policy's utility on those questions. Neither target is identified by
treating purchased draws or acquisition seeds as independent new questions.
The selected policy's in-sample maximum also has selection optimism.

G1 uses nine policies: always accept, seven training-confidence empirical
quantile boundaries, and always think. Quantile probabilities are
`1/8,...,7/8`, using the observed value at index `ceil(q*N)-1` after stable
sorting; remove duplicate action vectors, retain endpoints, and order policies
by escalation count. All comparisons use strict `<`. A utility tie, within
`1e-12`, selects the policy with fewer escalations, then the lower boundary.
This estimates the best member of this fixed class; it is not an optimization
guarantee over every real-valued threshold. Confidence is required to be finite.

The correctness–cost identity and variance identity are ordinary algebra:

```text
E[G_i] = E[Y_i] - D_i - beta*E[C_i]
Var(G_i | fixed state) = Var(Y_i) + beta^2*Var(C_i) - 2*beta*Cov(Y_i,C_i).
```

Dependence affects the noise and the cost trajectory, not the linear mean
identity. Positive gain is never assumed to be monotone in confidence.

## 2. Correct finite-pool estimation after adaptive, random-cost stopping

Reserve a small final random audit inside each checkpoint. Adaptive purchases
may depend arbitrarily on previously purchased correctness and lengths. Before
the audit, freeze the acquired set `O`, remaining set `M`, and one purchased-only
prediction `m_j` for every remaining cell. Let `m=|M|`, and choose
`L=min(4,m)` **before observing any audit outcome**. Buy a simple random sample
`A` of `L` remaining cells without replacement. Freeze the entire sample before
the first outcome is revealed; an early audit observation cannot change later
audit choices. Every sampled cell is purchased and charged.

For `m>0`, the estimator for any fixed threshold is

```text
J_hat(tau) = B_D
           + sum_{j in O} v_j*a_tau(i)*G_j
           + sum_{j in M} v_j*a_tau(i)*m_j
           + (m/L)*sum_{j in A} v_j*a_tau(i)*(G_j-m_j).
```

When `m=0`, use the exact observed cache total. Do not clip this estimator to
the feasible utility range: that would change its expectation. If `L=m`, the
formula is exact regardless of the predictions. All thresholds use the same
audit, retaining their shared covariance.

**Why the estimator is correct.** Condition on the entire adaptive pre-audit
history, the fixed cache, and frozen predictions. Every remaining cell has
inclusion probability `L/m`. Therefore the expectation of the correction is
exactly `sum_M v_j*a_tau(i)*(G_j-m_j)`. Adding the first three terms gives
`J_cache(tau)`. This holds even if random response lengths determined when the
adaptive phase stopped, and even if the prediction model is wrong. Independence
of cached outcomes is unnecessary for this finite-population sampling identity.
The audit randomizer must be independent of unrevealed labels and costs.

This is standard model-assisted sampling estimation, not a new adaptive
sampling theorem. For a fixed pair of thresholds, put
`e_j=v_j*(a_tau(i)-a_eta(i))*(G_j-m_j)`. Its conditional audit variance is
`m^2*(1-L/m)*S_e^2/L`, with finite-population variance denominator `m-1`.
For `L>=2`, the sample residual variance yields the ordinary design-unbiased
variance estimate. No normal approximation, calibrated confidence interval,
anytime coverage, or guarantee for the selected maximum is claimed here.

The random audit has a real opportunity cost. Hold `L*thinking_cap` tokens
aside; no refund inside this audit batch can buy extra draws. Match this audit
and final estimator across the seven acquisition controls. A baseline with its
own estimator requires a separately disclosed comparison.

### A counterexample to an unweighted adaptive average

Two equally weighted states have deterministic gains `+.8` and `-.8`, each
with two cells. Their finite-pool mean gain is zero. A strategy sees the first
state, then repeats it after its positive result. The unweighted acquired mean
is `+.8`; it has given one state all the weight. Averaging each observed state's
sample mean still returns `+.8` while the second state is unobserved.

Inverse one-step probabilities alone do not fix random stopping. With one state
and independent gains `+1/-1` at equal probability, buy one draw; stop after a
positive result, otherwise buy a second. The stopped sample mean has expectation
`.5*(1)+.5*(-.5)=+.25`, although the mean gain is zero. If negative outcomes are
longer, a token stop can produce the same dependence. Logging probabilities is
necessary for auditing a strategy but is not a proof that its stopped average
is unbiased. The reserved audit above avoids this particular problem.

## 3. A purchased-only acquisition heuristic

The following fixed score is intentionally small. Its pseudo-posterior
uncertainty is a purchase heuristic; the audit estimator supplies finite-pool
correction. Neither Gaussian shrinkage nor its plausible set is a confidence
set. Adaptive model fitting need not be unbiased for the correction to work.

Partition training confidence into four quantile strata, with the same
training-only boundary rule and strict comparisons. Preserve tied confidence
values in one stratum. `n_i` is the number of purchased cells at state `i`.
For each stratum, use purchased cells only to estimate mean correctness and
length, with beta-one pseudo-counts for correctness and two pseudo-cells of
length `thinking_cap/2`. The stratum prior gain for state `i` is
`m0_i=pY_stratum-D_i-beta*meanC_stratum`.

Use a normal mean model with prior between-state variance `b0=.10` and
within-state gain variance `h0=.20`, each shrunk with strength `nu=2` and
floored at `1e-4`. For within-state variance, each fully observed pair supplies

```text
h_pair = ((Y_1-Y_2)^2 + beta^2*(C_1-C_2)^2
          - 2*beta*(Y_1-Y_2)*(C_1-C_2))/2.
```

Pool these pairs within the stratum, with `nu*h0` in the numerator and
`nu+number_of_pairs` in the denominator. For between-state variance, use the
sample variance of the first purchased gain from each observed state in the
stratum minus the current within-state estimate, truncated below at zero;
shrink that value with `nu*b0` and weight `max(number_of_states-1,0)`.
These adaptively collected moments are model heuristics. They are not reported
as unbiased variance estimates. Clip predictions only, to state-specific gain
support `[-D_i-beta*thinking_cap, 1-D_i]`.

For `b=b_stratum`, `h=h_stratum`, define

```text
V_i(n) = 1/(1/b+n/h)
m_i(n) = V_i(n)*(m0_i/b + sum_purchased_G_i/h)
meanC_i = (2*meanC_stratum + sum_purchased_C_i)/(2+n_i).
```

Keep observed cells at their actual gains and put `m_j=m_i(n_i)` on unpurchased
cells. Compute the plug-in utility `J_model(tau)` using those values. Let
`P={tau: J_model(tau)>=max_eta J_model(eta)-.03}`. This is a fixed heuristic
plausibility tolerance. If it contains one policy, stop adaptive purchases for
scored methods at that checkpoint and conduct the reserved audit. This applies
to disagreement, information gain, reactive impact, the candidate, and both
removals. The three random controls continue until the cap or cache stops them.
There is no claim that the
unique policy is truly optimal.

If `r_i=R-n_i`, the model's remaining finite-cache contribution variance before
a purchase is proportional to `r_i^2*V_i(n_i)+r_i*h`. The estimated reduction
from one more draw is

```text
Delta_i = (w_i/R)^2 * [r_i^2*V_i(n_i)+r_i*h
             - (r_i-1)^2*V_i(n_i+1)-(r_i-1)*h]
K_i = sum_{unordered tau,eta in P} (a_tau(i)-a_eta(i))^2
score_i = K_i*Delta_i / max(meanC_i,1).
```

This uses shared policy differences and compares coverage with a repeat in the
same score. It does not require a monotone gain curve. If all eligible scores
are zero, select uniformly unless `P` is a singleton. Otherwise assign `.9`
probability to the maximum-score state (stable question order breaks ties) and
`.1` to a uniform draw among eligible states. For selected state `i`, choose
uniformly among its unpurchased cells. Log exact conditional state and cell
probabilities, eligible sets, scores, predictions, and reservation before
revealing correctness or length. The audit estimator does not depend on these
adaptive probabilities; retaining them permits independent leakage review.

Two fixed removals retain every other parameter. `candidate_no_repeat` limits
**adaptive** acquisitions to previously unobserved states; the common seed and
random audits may still supply repeats. `candidate_no_joint_covariance` deletes
only the cross term in the variance model used for the purchase reduction
`Delta_i`. At a fixed purchased history, compute all mean predictions, the
plausible policy set, and the audit predictions with the full joint model for
both versions. Use the alternate within-state variance and its corresponding
between-state variance only in `V_i` and `Delta_i` for the removal. Otherwise
the changed variance would also alter the shrinkage mean and confound the
component comparison. Retain the same outcomes, cost means, gain means and
final estimator at that fixed history. Later histories may differ because
purchases differ. This tests covariance in purchase uncertainty, not a changed
utility target.

## 4. Costs and exact checkpoint stop

The revealed budget charges all common direct states, common thinking seed
cells, adaptive cells, and audits. Hidden future cost is never read to determine
affordability. Before each purchase, reserve its full cap; expose `(Y,C)` only
after the reservation. Settle to actual charge and return unused reservation
only after that purchase. Failures would retain conservative charges, but this
synthetic protocol contains no runtime-failure simulation.

At checkpoint `B`, reserve `min(4,m)*thinking_cap` for the final audit. An
adaptive purchase is affordable only if its full cap plus the audit reservation
after that purchase fits the checkpoint. If at most four cells remain, audit
all of them. If none remain, reuse the exact cache estimate. No unpurchased cost
can influence a reservation or policy. If the seed plus audit cannot fit, fail
the protocol instead of silently shrinking the seed/audit.

Checkpoints are nested. An earlier audit is ordinary purchased training data
at the next checkpoint, and all its charges persist. Each checkpoint freezes
its policy and trace before later purchases. A unique plausible policy may
stop an adaptive phase early, but the next checkpoint may revisit acquisition
after the preceding audit. Cache exhaustion carries the exact current policy
forward. This differs from independently restarting a strategy at each budget.

Keep three reports separate: revealed strategy expenditure; every response
physically generated for a cache, including unused responses; and deployment
cost. Synthetic lengths stand for revealed token cost, not model compute or
end-to-end latency. Audits and direct-state costs remain visible in the curves.

```text
for method and acquisition seed:
    expose/charge ALL direct states; construct training-only policy grid
    buy the shared 8 distinct-state seed cells, reserving each full cap
    for B in [64000,128000,256000]:
        while adaptive cells are eligible and audit reserve can be retained:
            build model from purchased history only
            compute method score; optionally stop at unique plausible policy
            sample state/cell with logged probabilities
            reserve full thinking cap; buy hidden cell; settle actual cost
        freeze O,M,m_predictions and full random audit sample
        reserve/buy/settle every audit cell without adapting inside the batch
        compute J_hat for ALL thresholds from the frozen pre-audit model
        freeze minimum-escalation maximizer and complete checkpoint trace
    return immutable policies and logs
only an evaluator then computes held-out utility and full-cache audit error
```

## 5. Seven acquisition controls and prior-method mapping

All controls share direct exposure, eight-cell seed, threshold grid, final audit,
budget reservations, tie handling and evaluation. Scored controls also share the
singleton plausible-set stop. Random controls use the affordable budget.
The comparison with random controls tests acquisition and stopping together.
The information-gain comparison holds this stopping rule fixed. Report actual
spending as well as policy quality available by each allowed budget.

| Implementation | Purchase rule | Status and nearest overlap |
|---|---|---|
| `uniform_coverage` | Uniform unseen states first; then uniform eligible states. | Standard coverage control. |
| `uniform_repeat` | Finish the second cell of a uniformly chosen one-cell state before opening another state. | Fixed repeat schedule control. |
| `confidence_stratified` | Uniform among nonempty confidence strata, then uniform eligible states in that stratum. | Standard stratified allocation. |
| `disagreement` | Maximize `K_i`, with matched exploration; no uncertainty/cost division. | Disagreement-based active learning adaptation. |
| `closest_cost_aware` | Global-threshold information score per predicted token. | L1-inspired adaptation described below; no faithful reproduction claim. |
| `reactive_impact` | Expected fraction of training threshold decisions changed after one hypothetical gain observation. | L2 utility-impact adaptation; no annotator-majority semantics. |
| `candidate` | `K_i*Delta_i/meanC_i`. | Cost-aware disagreement/variance-reduction adaptation. |

For `closest_cost_aware`, use 32 fixed pseudo-posterior particles of state means
`mu_mi=m_i+sqrt(V_i)*z_mi`, clipped to gain support. A particle selects its best
global training-pool threshold using deterministic ties. Keep acquired cells
at their actual gains. Apply each particle mean only to the remaining cells:
its threshold total is the direct base plus the observed-cell sum plus
`sum_i (R-n_i)/(N*R)*a_tau(i)*mu_mi`. This matches the candidate's finite-cache
target. These latent mean particles omit remaining-cell predictive noise and
are a heuristic, not a full posterior for finite-cache totals. Convert the mean into
a predicted correctness probability
`p_mi=clip(mu_mi+D_i+beta*meanC_i,.001,.999)`. Estimate purchased-only probabilities
of high/low length conditional on correctness within each confidence stratum,
with beta-one pseudo-counts; high means `C>thinking_cap/2`. Four categories
`o=(Y,high_length)` give a finite probability table `q_mi(o)`. Estimate
`p(T=k,o)` by averaging `1[T_m=k]*q_mi(o)` over particles, then score the mutual
information `I(T;o)/meanC_i`. Common particle normals are fixed before outcomes
and reused; there is no adaptive Monte Carlo seed search.

This finite categorical model is a **heuristic surrogate**, not a calibrated
posterior for actual continuous lengths. Its purchased gain update remains the
matched Gaussian moment update above. L1's original received-context action
objective becomes a global finite-threshold pool objective, with repeats and
random reservations. Those changes preclude claiming original-paper guarantees
or a faithful implementation. G0's full-paper comparison must retain them.

For `reactive_impact`, freeze five hypothetical gains
`m_i+sqrt(h)*[-2,-1,0,1,2]`, clipped to support, with weights
`[.05,.25,.40,.25,.05]`. Update only that state's mean/variance for each scenario,
choose the new model-best threshold, and measure the fraction of training
actions changed from the current model-best threshold. Score the weighted
fraction; do not divide by cost in this fixed control. This is the expected
impact principle applied to stochastic utility, not repeated annotation of one
latent correct label. Computation overhead is part of the report.

L3's action-level independent feedback-graph observation and stopping guarantee
do not transfer automatically to correlated nested threshold rewards and a
stochastic token stop. L5's propensity-based counterfactual learning also does
not license an unweighted stopped mean. The present audit is a design-based
finite-pool correction. The literature comparison decides whether replacing
another control by an applicable L3 adaptation is warranted before G1 freeze;
the seven-method cap cannot expand after outcomes are seen.

## 6. An analytical purchase example

Consider two eligible states inside the disagreement region, both with
`K_i=1`, equal pool weights, and two cells per state. Scale out the common
`(w_i/R)^2`. State A has one observed cell, `b=.10`, `h=.40`, so
`V_A(1)=.08`, `V_A(2)=.0666667`; its next-cell reduction is `.48`. Its predicted
cost is 200 tokens. State B is unseen, `b=.04`, `h=.01`, so
`V_B(0)=.04`, `V_B(1)=.008`; its reduction is
`4*.04+.01-.008=.162`. Its predicted cost is 100 tokens.
The candidate scores A as `.48/200=.0024` and B as `.162/100=.00162`, choosing
a repeat of A on the exploitation branch. Coverage chooses B. The no-cost
disagreement control ties and follows stable state order. An information-gain
control may make either choice according to its full threshold uncertainty.
This demonstrates that two purchase contracts can differ; it does not show
that the choice is new, correct, or useful on real data. No experiment ran.

## 7. Bounded G1 contract and remaining blockers

Proposed implementation defaults are `N=64`, `R=2`, independent evaluation
`N_eval=512`, `R_eval=2`, direct/thinking caps `512/2562`, eight distinct seed
states, four confidence strata, at most nine thresholds, audit four cells,
exploration `.1`, plausibility width `.03`, 32 particles, and the priors above.
Use 30 paired synthetic seeds, seven controls, the two fixed removals and three
nested checkpoints. The driver must freeze a protocol/source digest and write
immutable output with a full purchase trace and a bounded runtime.

Eight synthetic IDs overlap the plan's stress families without adding cases:
`null_no_benefit`, `threshold_cost_zero`, `high_noise_repeat`,
`heterogeneous_nonmonotonic`, `rare_rescue_harms`, `high_direct_cost`,
`threshold_cost_positive`, and `threshold_cost_negative`. The nonmonotonic case
also has strong question differences. The three threshold-cost cases have
identical marginal DGPs for correctness and length; only their dependence
changes. Their finite realized marginals need not match exactly. Mechanism
screens are predesignated for `high_noise_repeat` and
`threshold_cost_negative`, retaining every null/adverse case.

G1 still requires an actual reviewed source pin and machine-readable protocol,
exact synthetic DGPs/seeds, independent estimator/accessor/accounting tests,
the fixed practical screen and runtime/output cap, and explicit agreement that
the L1/L2 controls are adaptations. Four audit draws can have substantial
variance; simulations may close this recipe. The finite-cache unbiasedness
identity cannot establish an unseen-question benefit or selected-policy
coverage. A new real-data endpoint, roles, scoring access and policy/evaluation
lock are required before G2. This document allocates no real questions and
authorizes no generation or completed-study operation.
