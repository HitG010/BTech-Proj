# Qwen3-0.6B sparse guard: baseline refit and question-only preparation

This additive protocol prepares one **fresh diagnostic** of a cheap parser guard around a plain-confidence router. It does not implement or authorize a collector. The original 64-question opportunity panel, its completion, scores, and failed original wall-budget result remain unchanged. The failed cached three-feature learner is not the object of this validation.

Freeze this document, `scripts/qwen06_sparse_validation.py`, and its tests before running `prepare-policy`. The script checks the closed 64-question, two-draw Qwen3-0.6B pilot through its frozen `checked_plan`, including the 2,652-file parent-study inventory and pilot plan/rows/report/source pins. It uses the 128 existing paired training rows only. One scalar threshold is selected from `−∞`, unique postwarper direct confidences, and `+∞` to maximize mean training incremental utility `1[confidence < τ] × (Ythinking − Ydirect − 0.05 × thinking_tokens/1000)`. Ties choose fewer escalations, then the lower threshold. `never` and `always` serialize the infinities. The fit-start marker prevents a second fit. The **same** threshold defines two frozen prospective policies: plain confidence, and plain confidence OR gold-free numeric-v7 direct parser rejection. There is no feature search, new learner, or use of fresh outcomes to select the threshold.

After independent review of `policy.json`, write a checksummed `policy-review.json` in `qwen06-sparse-validation-v1/` with payload `{"policy_sha256":"<checked policy digest>","decision":"independently_reviewed_allow_question_only_preparation"}`. Only then may `prepare-questions` run. If the fitted threshold is `always`, both policies are structurally identical for finite fresh confidences, so preparation refuses before selecting or earmarking any reserve questions. A finite threshold with no changed training rows does not trigger this guard because fresh questions may differ. Otherwise, preparation verifies all reserve-child sibling manifests and live allocation/source/exclusion/prompt identities. It chooses 228 hash-ordered questions with seed 20261018 from the unclaimed parent reserve (484 initially after the old 64 child), disjoint from every other role and sibling. New child `reserve-children/qwen06-guard-fresh228-v1/` uses split `guard_fresh228`, retains parent row provenance, has only question records, and requires `gold_sha256: null` with no gold file. Parent allocation and old child bytes do not change. The preparation creates 228 direct plus 228 **potential** thinking job identities with a new seed 20261019 and one sample each. Model/revision, prompt, decoder, caps, scorer identity, and local backend metadata come from the frozen Qwen3-0.6B arm; no model is constructed. The question protocol remains `collection_unimplemented_locked`.

A separately frozen future collector would first complete all 228 direct responses. It must validate each full generation result against the frozen job and runner's `valid_record_result` before eligibility; the pure eligibility helper additionally rejects duplicate questions, nonzero samples, unfinished rows, and timeout terminations. Gold-free v7 extraction then freezes the complete eligibility mask: parser rejection **and** acceptance by the plain-confidence threshold. More than 32 eligible questions is a hard resource stop; no subsampling or reference access. For at most 32, only those thinking jobs may run. Proposed response-only ceiling is `228×512 + 32×(2048+512+2) = 198,720` tokens; proposed global ceilings are 250,000 tokens and six hours, setup 900 seconds, job 600 seconds, sampled RSS 9 GiB, one initial invocation plus at most one explicit resume, two setups, first-failure stop, and no automatic resume. These are planning limits, not an implemented runtime authorization. Full 228 direct plus all eligible thinking, exited collector, accounting, and preservation checks must precede **only eligible selected training-reference** access; zero eligibility means zero references. Nothing in this prep-only module fetches references or generates responses.

The future sparse comparison uses zero increment for each unchanged question. For eligible questions, direct numeric-v7 parser rejection fixes direct correctness at zero, so `Δq = Ythinking − 0.05 × thinking_tokens/1000`. Report the 228-question mean, 95% whole-question bootstrap interval (2,000 draws, seed 20261018), accuracy increment, extra thinking tokens per original question, event rate, and conditional thinking accuracy. The predeclared further-study screen is point gain at least +.01 and lower 95% bound above zero. These are diagnostic fixed-action estimates; no absolute full-policy accuracy/utility, always-thinking counterfactual, confirmation, or novelty claim follows. This fresh sample validates the frozen cheap guard hypothesis, not the previously reused 64-question learner.
